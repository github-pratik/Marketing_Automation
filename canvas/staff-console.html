What a pull actually does

Searching is free. Addresses cost. Preview runs Apollo's free search — no credits, as often as you like. Pull spends one lead credit per person to reveal the email. That is why they are two buttons and not one.
Pulled people are not emailed. They land in exactly the same queue as a typed lead: verified, de-duplicated against everyone we have ever contacted, checked against the never-contact list, then sent within the daily limit. A pull of 25 with 3 sends left today means 22 wait their turn — it does not mean 25 emails.
25 is the cap per run, on purpose. A broad filter can match tens of thousands of people. Without a ceiling, one wide search and one confident click would spend the whole credit balance in a second.
The engine behind this screen is live. Search and pull both run for real on the server: a search with these filters returned 490 matching people for one US state at 51–200 staff, for zero credits. What is not connected is this page — a published prototype cannot call our server, so the results below come from a sample set. Connecting it is a hosting step, not a build step.
