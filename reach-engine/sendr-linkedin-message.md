# Sendr LinkedIn campaign copy — OryonIQ (draft)

Two-step sequence, matching the same signal/offer/CTA as the email leg
(`config-oryoniq.json`) and the personalized page (`sendr-page-template.md`), so a prospect
who sees more than one channel gets a consistent story.

## Step 1 — Connection request note

LinkedIn hard-caps connection notes at **300 characters**. No pitch here — just a specific,
human reason to connect.

> Hi {{first_name}} — following the CMMC Phase II news and DoD's AI/cloud push this year, figured
> it's a good time to connect with capture folks in the GovCon space. Wanted to say hello.

(≈195 characters — leaves headroom for longer first names.)

## Step 2 — Follow-up message (after they accept)

No hard LinkedIn limit on a regular 1st-degree message, but Sendr strips formatting and long
messages read as spam — keep it under ~700 characters, same discipline as the email opener.

> {{opener}}
>
> The Pentagon suspended CMMC Phase II on July 13, 2026, and DoD committed $32B to AI, cloud, and
> cyber in H1 FY2026 — set-asides are opening fast.
>
> OryonIQ flags the pursuits that fit your NAICS and SDB status before the RFP drops. Put
> together a quick page with the ones it's surfacing for {{company}}: {{page_url}}
>
> Ellen, OryonIQ / VisioneerIT

## Variables needed

Same tag convention as the page template — declare these in Sendr's campaign message editor via
its own insert-variable control:

| Tag | Source |
|---|---|
| `first_name` | lead's first name |
| `company` | lead's company |
| `opener` | AI-written per-lead line (same source as email + page) |
| `page_url` | the Sendr personalized page URL generated by `push_to_sendr_page.py` for this lead — generate the page first, then feed its URL back in as this campaign's variable |

## Sequencing note

Build this as a 2-step Sendr campaign (connection request → wait for accept → follow-up), not a
single message — LinkedIn's own limits force the split, and it also reads less like automation.
