# OryonIQ — product feature list (source of truth for copy)

Compiled 2026-08-10 from the live public site: https://www.oryoniq.com/
Use this when writing any OryonIQ offer, CTA, page, or LinkedIn copy. **Do not promise a
capability that is not on this list** — see the open question at the bottom.

## Positioning (as the site states it)

- Headline: **"AI-Powered Relationships That Drive Government Contracting Success"**
- FAQ framing: market intelligence by *"visualizing government opportunities and mapping it
  to social data"* — opportunity data joined to relationship data.
- Audience: federal contractors, both newcomers and seasoned GovCon professionals.
- Claimed outcomes: higher win rates, increased target insights, competitor intelligence.

## Modules

### Orbit — networking engine
- AI-Suggested Matches
- Smart Fit Signals
- Built-In Chat & Response Insights

### Constellation — community / teaming
- Professional community collaboration; "micro-connections" between members
- Instant answers from peers
- Team management with reports
- LinkedIn profile mapping — trace your connections into decision-makers

### Competitive Intelligence
- Contract award data: who is winning, who is teaming with whom
- Prime supplier lists / supplier data search
- Opportunity data visualized against social/relationship data

### Events & Resources
- GovCon industry event discovery ("Build Connection IRL")
- Living library of insights, lessons learned, peer wisdom
- State/local programs, mentorship, associations ("Your Strategic Advantage")

### OryonAI
- Chatbot
- AI Matchmaking

## ⚠️ Open question — copy/product mismatch

The outbound copy currently sells a capability that does **not** appear anywhere on the site:

| Where | Claim |
|---|---|
| `config-oryoniq.json` → `one_liner` | "surfaces federal pursuits before the RFP" |
| `config-oryoniq.json` → `offer` | "flags the pursuits that fit your NAICS and SDB status before the RFP drops" |
| `config-oryoniq.json` → `sendr.gif_hyperlink_text` | "See the 3 pursuits OryonIQ is tracking for your NAICS codes" |
| `campaign-oryoniq-pilot.json` → CTA | "Want the three it's surfacing for a firm like yours?" |

No pre-RFP alerting, NAICS filtering, or SDB-status matching is described in any public
material. The product's actual centre of gravity is **network, teaming, and competitive
intelligence**, not an early-warning opportunity feed.

Resolve before the next live send — either the site undersells the product, or the copy
oversells it. If the latter, the strongest *true* reframe is teaming: with CMMC Phase II
suspended (2026-07-13) every bid field just widened, so knowing who to team with and who is
already winning beats another opportunity feed.
